# StealthRead

StealthRead is a TeamsPlus-exclusive utility tool allowing you to read messages sent by other people **without** letting them know you ever read it. Unlike the official implementation in Microsoft Teams (which may be unavailable depending on your organization policies), StealthRead allows asymmetrical read receipts meaning you can see other's read receipts while they cannot see yours.

StealthRead does not require your organization to give explicit permission to disable read receipts.

!!! warning
    StealthRead may occasionally be unreliable due to some temporary issues or changes on Microsoft's side. Be aware if your life depends on others not seeing your messages.

    **We are not responsible for any consequences caused by the occasional failures of StealthRead to block outbound read receipts.**

    Do not allow the occasional unreliability of StealthRead to ruin your life.

## Activation Steps

1. Go to the Chats tab in Microsoft Teams
2. The *StealthRead* button should be at the top. If it isn't, try refreshing the page.
3. Click to toggle it. It should say "StealthRead" when disabled, and "StealthRead Enabled" when enabled.
4. Refresh the application to apply your changes.

!!! note
    Activating StealthRead may cause Microsoft Teams to constantly notify you about "connection issues". This is normal when StealthRead is enabled. These notifications usually go away after you disable StealthRead.
